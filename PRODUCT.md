# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who dislike loud, novelty, or embarrassing default ringtones and want a simple sound for calls and notifications. They are likely visiting from a phone or desktop while choosing a ringtone and may need concise platform-specific setup guidance.

## Product Purpose

Sober Ringtones offers minimal, cringe-free ringtones and notification sounds, plus practical iOS and Android guides for installing them. Success means visitors quickly understand the premise, preview/download a sound, and trust that the project is modest, useful, and easy to share.

## Brand Personality

Humble, dry, minimal. The voice should feel plainspoken and lightly opinionated: a small useful project for people who want their phone to stop making a scene.

## Anti-references

Avoid ringtone-store flashiness, novelty-sound chaos, aggressive promotion, and generic app-landing-page gloss. The site should not feel loud, gamified, or overexplained.

## Design Principles

- Keep the joke quiet: the premise can be funny without turning the interface into a gag.
- Make the useful path obvious: preview, download, and setup guidance should stay easy to find.
- Preserve restraint: small controls, clear typography, and purposeful color should carry the experience.
- Respect the device context: mobile reading and touch targets matter because the product ultimately lives on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, clear link labels, and sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes. Honor reduced-motion preferences when adding animation.
